About Us

Steel Detailing Built for Fabricators & Erectors

We are a specialist steel detailing company focused on accelerating fabrication and simplifying erection through precise, fabrication-ready detailing solutions. With over 500+ successfully delivered projects and 10+ years of experience, we provide high-quality shop drawings, erection drawings, and 3D modeling using Tekla Structures and AutoCAD.

Our team specializes in PEMB, structural steel, joists & decking, warehouses, cold storage facilities, and storage buildings. By bridging the gap between design and execution, we ensure seamless coordination, zero-error detailing, and faster project delivery. We are committed to precision, rapid turnaround, and dependable support that fabricators and erectors can rely on with confidence.
